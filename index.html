Questions

Things families ask us first

The ones that come up in almost every first meeting — answered the way we'd answer them across the desk.

Still have a question? Ask a counsellor directly — no form, no wait.

WhatsApp us
Do you charge for the first consultation?
No. The first consultation is free and carries no obligation. We only charge a service fee once you decide to proceed with applications, and that figure is agreed in writing beforehand.
What if my IELTS score isn't ready yet?
That's normal, and it doesn't stop us starting. Many universities issue conditional offers pending your score.
  • We shortlist courses while you prepare
  • In-house IELTS and PTE coaching available
  • Conditional offers converted once your score arrives
Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?
No, and you should be careful with anyone who does. Visa decisions rest with the embassy. What we can do is make sure your file is complete, consistent and well-evidenced — which is why our approval rate sits at 98%.
How much money do I need to show?
It varies by country, course length and whether you have dependants. As a rough guide, most Australian applications require one year of tuition plus living costs held for a defined period. We calculate your exact figure in the first meeting.
Do you help after I arrive?
Yes. Accommodation is confirmed before you fly, airport pickup is arranged, and your counsellor stays reachable through your first semester for bank account, SIM and part-time work questions.
Which countries do you work with?
Australia, the United Kingdom, Canada, the USA, Malaysia and Germany. We are official representatives for more than 120 institutions across those six.
My CGPA is low. Is it worth applying?
Often yes — pathway programmes, diplomas with credit transfer and foundation routes exist for exactly this. We'll tell you plainly whether your profile has a realistic route, or whether waiting a year is the better call.
Can I work while studying?
Most student visas allow limited part-time work during term and full-time during breaks, but the caps differ by country and change periodically. We cover the current rules for your destination in the pre-departure briefing.
