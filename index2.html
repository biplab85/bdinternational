How it works

Three steps, the same for every student we take on.

  1. Step 01

    Free counselling. Bring your transcripts and budget; leave with a shortlist and an honest read on your chances.

  2. Step 02

    Application and visa. We file it — financial papers, GTE statement and all — and review everything twice.

  3. Step 03

    Departure and arrival. Briefing, forex, SIM and pickup, plus a contact you can call in your first month.
